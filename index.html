Overview

A brand made of light and liquid

VOLT is a fictional energy drink I designed to explore a bold, futuristic identity and an experimental way of advertising a product. The launch poster puts a matte-black can at the center of a burst of chrome-bright liquid, ice crystals and electric light.

The goal was to make the product recognizable at a glance while keeping the visual energy high.

The challenge

How do you show energy, intensity and a futuristic personality without burying the product?

The poster had to feel intense and experimental, yet keep a clear order: the can first, the VOLT name second, supporting copy last.

It also had to hold up as a small preview in a social feed, where only the strongest shapes and colors survive.

Creative direction

Cyber Energy, built on Liquid Experiments

Everything on the poster comes from one idea: a product that looks like it was dropped into electrified water.

Liquid type

A slanted wordmark with a glossy liquid finish and a V-shaped swoosh that flows into the letters.

Material contrast

A matte-black can with real condensation, set against glowing liquid and glass-like ice.

Cyan-led color

Electric cyan leads, purple adds depth, and white keeps the small text readable.

Frozen motion

A forward-tilted can, curling liquid arcs and flying droplets create movement in a still image.

Visual identity

One name, one glow, four colors

The logo pairs a bold, slanted wordmark with a custom V-shaped liquid swoosh. A soft cyan glow makes it look lit from within. The same mark sits large at the bottom of the poster and on the can.

Design principles

Four rules that kept the chaos readable

Product first

The can is the biggest and brightest element. Its matte body stays distinct from the glossy effects around it.

Controlled chaos

Splashes and ice are dense, while the corners and the area under the logo stay calm for text.

Readable when small

The VOLT name appears twice at high contrast. Supporting copy is small and secondary.

Material contrast

Real-looking aluminum, droplets and ice meet glowing, surreal liquid for a premium feel.

Thumbnail test

The poster at three feed sizes. At the smallest, the brand still reads as a dark can with a lit cyan V.

Design process

From idea to final poster

  1. Define the brandCreated the fictional brand, its message and the Cyber Energy direction.
  2. Set the identityChose the palette, type style and the V-shaped logo concept.
  3. Build the assetsDeveloped the can, liquid, ice and droplet elements.
  4. ComposePlaced the can, liquid arcs and foreground elements into one dynamic layout.
  5. RefineTuned typography, contrast, lighting and hierarchy.
  6. Test at small sizeChecked the poster at full size and at thumbnail size before finishing.
Tools and workflow
Adobe PhotoshopAdobe IllustratorAI-assisted exploration

Photoshop handled compositing, lighting, texture and final assembly. Illustrator handled vector logo and geometric elements. My manual focus was composition, type placement, hierarchy, color balance, legibility and final quality control.

Outcome

Loud on color, calm where it counts.

The final poster gives VOLT a distinctive, high-energy look. The brand name and the can read instantly, the cyan and purple palette stays consistent from logo to splash, and the glossy liquid contrasts strongly with the matte can.

What I took from it: an experimental style works when it has one clear hero, a limited palette and a quiet area for text.
